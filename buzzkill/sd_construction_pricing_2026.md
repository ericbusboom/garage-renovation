**Number: $63.40–$68.08/hr base wage; $98.75–$103.43/hr total (Operating Engineer)**
- Source: CA DIR 2026-1 Southern CA (HARD published rate)
- URL: https://www.dir.ca.gov/OPRL/2026-1/PWD/Southern.html
- PDF: https://www.dir.ca.gov/OPRL/2026-1/PWD/Determinations/Southern/SC-023-63-2.pdf
- Key finding: NO separate Ironworker determination in 2026-1 Southern CA. Steel erection falls under Operating Engineer (Groups 1-25) or Carpenter ($52.24/hr base, $81.32/hr total)
- Carpenter PDF: https://www.dir.ca.gov/OPRL/2026-1/PWD/Determinations/Southern/SC-023-31-2.pdf
- Uncertainty: MODERATE (rates are hard, classification is ambiguous)
