# 04 — Geotechnical and Foundations

This section establishes the ground conditions and parameters needed to design new columns, piers, footings, slabs, and any retained foundation reliance. It must address fill, expansive soil, groundwater, settlement, lateral resistance, uplift, corrosion, mapped hazards, excavation, and drainage as applicable.

Early assumptions such as 18-inch-diameter by 36-inch-deep piers are not design values.

## Required outputs

- geotechnical report or documented City-acceptable basis for not requiring one
- foundation design criteria
- excavation, observation, testing, and special-inspection requirements
- foundation schedule coordinated with structural reactions
