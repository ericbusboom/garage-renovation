# 13 — Record Documents

This section is populated during permitting and construction. Preserve received approvals, signed reports, inspection records, and manufacturer documents in their original form.

## Required outputs

- approved permits and agency correspondence
- RFIs, submittals, shop drawings, substitutions, and design clarifications
- inspection, special-inspection, test, and commissioning records
- change orders and revision history
- as-built drawings/models and final photographs
- warranties, maintenance instructions, equipment manuals, and closeout approvals
