# Chapter 8 figure set

New figures belong to SOL-001 revision 1, draft, 2026-09-15. PNGs support inline review; matching PDFs preserve vector output for sharing.

- `seasonal-production`: monthly output at three pitches against modeled demand.
- `seasonal-tradeoff`: winter and summer production at fixed 6 kW capacity.
- `west-sections`: dimensioned roof-envelope comparison at fixed 325 ft² face.
- `geometry-tradeoff`: fixed face versus fixed footprint.
- `module-layout`: illustrative twelve-module fit, 5.64 kW; no approved pathways/clearances.
- `battery-sizing`: utility-bill comparisons for one or two batteries and PV-only.
- `battery-operation`: synthetic January/August hourly averages for 6 kW at 35°.

Files beginning `historical-` are unchanged older figures. Their original diagrams may use superseded geometry or labels. The chapter explains each limitation. They remain examples of the analysis history, not standalone current instructions.
