The chapter consolidates the prior solar, battery, weather, consumption and financial work, and adds 30°, 35° and 40° comparisons. Its planning recommendation is approximately **5.6–6.0 kW DC with one 13.5 kWh battery**, developing **35°** alongside the 30° fallback. This is a recommendation for review, not an adopted design decision.

The current area budget is **325 ft² gross solar face**. The older studies used approximately **401 ft²**; their capacity/roof-fit conclusions are labeled historical. Gross roof area is not generating module area or net loft floor area. Final module packing, access clearances, cap/window height, shade, tariff/provider and contractor quotes remain open.

The calculations use cached PVGIS/ERA5 inputs for continuity, with current official utility, incentive, product and permit references checked on 2026-09-15. The financial model remains a planning approximation. Reproduction instructions are in `../../solar-study/chapter8/README.md` and in the source ZIP.

This section coordinates the solar resource and economics with the actual roof, structure, fire access, waterproofing, electrical service, battery location, and utility requirements. Active PV modules and visually matching inactive infill panels must be distinguishable in technical documents.
