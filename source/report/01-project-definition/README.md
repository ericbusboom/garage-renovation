# 01 — Project Definition

This section states what is being built and why. It will contain the owner project brief, legal use of the loft and garage, scope and exclusions, selected concept, design criteria, equipment/storage requirements, appearance goals, budget, schedule goals, and a coordinated basis of design.

Do not bury new decisions in drawings. Record them in the project-controls decision log and incorporate them into the basis of design.

## Required outputs

- `project-brief.md`
- `basis-of-design.pdf`
- use/space and equipment schedule
- responsibility matrix covering architect, structural, geotechnical, electrical/solar, civil, contractor, and delegated design
