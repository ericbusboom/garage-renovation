# ENV-003 — Infill wall panel options

**Revision:** 0 · **Date:** 2026-09-17 · **Status:** `draft`
**Purpose:** Owner-directed materials and market survey for the inside-frame wall panels.

## Read the study

- [Wall panel options study](wall-panel-options.md) — panel families and costs, the continuous-face versus bay-infill decision, whether panels can supply lateral stiffness, glazed-bay candidates, marine corrosion, and fire-code triggers.

## What this is

A research document requested by the owner. It surveys published manufacturer and trade-press material and compares it against the frame geometry recorded in [STR-006](../../06-structural-engineering/frame-geometry-study/README.md) revision 0 and the working cost basis in [COST-ESTIMATE.md](../../../COST-ESTIMATE.md).

It is **not** a product selection, a structural design, a code determination, or a quotation. Every price is a published market range.

## Findings that change what has been assumed

1. **No bay clear width is a whole multiple of any standard panel width** (30, 36, 42, 44 in). Tongue-and-groove and concealed-clip panels therefore suit continuous inside-face installation, not bay-by-bay infill. This constrains the attachment decision more than appearance does.
2. **Insulated metal panels cannot serve as shear walls.** Published manufacturer and trade guidance is explicit that IMPs are cladding, not structural support. If the 2-inch IMP in the current cost basis is retained, the rigidity question is closed and the frame carries all lateral load.
3. **Profiled single-skin sheet can be designed as a wall diaphragm** under ANSI/AISI S310 and SDI DDM04, with published test capacity from roughly 500 to 2,300 plf ASD. The study argues against pursuing it here, because it forecloses glazing in any bay it occupies and needs close, visible edge fastener spacing.
4. **The site is in a marine corrosion zone.** Published Galvalume warranties exclude structures within 1,500 ft of salt water, with one manufacturer at two miles. Painted aluminum is the warrantable substrate inside that zone. The distance to mean high tide for both the ocean and Mission Bay must be established from the survey before any substrate is specified.
5. **The 5/8" Type X drywall in the estimate may be doing double duty** as the IBC Chapter 26 thermal barrier for a foam-core panel. It should not be deleted as redundant insulation without re-establishing that function.
6. **Cost spread across panel families is roughly $2,000 to $18,500 installed** over the recorded 665 sq ft. The choice is aesthetic and durability-driven, not thermal, because the cavity wall behind can deliver the insulation either way.
