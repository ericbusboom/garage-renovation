# 07 — Building Envelope

This section turns the exposed-frame, inside-panel concept into a weatherproof and durable assembly. It covers the integrated solar roof, low hip cap, metal wall panels, insulation, thermal bridges, condensation, ventilation, waterproofing, flashing, drainage, soffit/fascia, openings, corrosion protection, and transitions to retained construction.

## Required outputs

- envelope basis and assembly schedule
- roof, wall, opening, base, edge, hip, balcony, and transition details
- drainage plan and overflow strategy
- condensation/ventilation analysis
- product data, compatibility record, warranties, and mockup/testing criteria
