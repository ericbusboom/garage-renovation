# Document Requirements and Acceptance Criteria

This is the content specification for the project report. It distinguishes a useful design-development archive from a package that can support professional review, permitting, bidding, construction, and closeout.

## 1. Project definition and design basis

Required content:

- owner program and intended legal use of every level and outdoor area;
- project scope, demolition scope, retained construction, and work by others;
- adopted dimensions, elevations, orientation, property datum, and coordinate system;
- occupancy, construction type, fire-sprinkler status, accessibility applicability, and code editions, as determined by the design professional;
- performance criteria for storage, equipment, hoist/trolley, solar generation, weather protection, ventilation, durability, appearance, cost, and construction sequence;
- explicit exclusions and future work;
- decision log, assumptions register, open-items register, and design responsibility matrix.

Acceptance: the selected scheme and every controlling criterion are identifiable without reading chat history.

## 2. Site survey and existing conditions

Required content:

- signed boundary/topographic survey with benchmarks and property corners;
- lot dimensions, easements, rights-of-way, setbacks or building restriction lines, and encroachments;
- footprints, roof outlines, heights, finished floors, grades, drainage, retaining walls, fences, paved areas, and adjacent structures;
- overhead and underground utilities, service equipment, meters, poles, wires, clearances, sewer/water routes, and utility easements;
- trees and large vegetation with trunk, canopy, approximate root-protection area, health concerns, and public/private status;
- measured plans, elevations, sections, and roof framing of the existing garage and related outbuilding/walkway;
- photo log keyed to a site plan;
- condition assessment of slab, walls, foundations, framing, corrosion, moisture, cracking, and previous alterations;
- existing permit and inspection record; hazardous-material screening before demolition where applicable.

Acceptance: proposed work can be located and demolition/connections can be designed without relying on an unverified sketch.

## 3. Land use, zoning, and permit report

Required content:

- assessor parcel number, ownership/title reference, legal description, and recorded easements;
- base zone, community plan designation, and all overlays;
- Coastal Overlay Zone and coastal-development-permit determination;
- height limit, setbacks, structure separation, lot coverage, floor-area ratio, parking, access, and use limitations;
- status of the existing garage and prior additions;
- fire-separation-distance/opening limitations near lot lines;
- environmental, flood, steep-slope, geologic-hazard, historic, airport, parking, transit, and brush/fire screenings as applicable;
- required permits, discretionary actions, utility approvals, deferred submittals, and responsible agencies;
- code summary and documented interpretations or pre-application meetings.

Acceptance: each conclusion cites the controlling map, code section, agency record, or written interpretation and states its verification date.

## 4. Geotechnical and foundation basis

Required content:

- mapped geologic hazards and site reconnaissance;
- subsurface investigation or documented basis for using permitted presumptive values;
- fill, expansive soil, groundwater, corrosion, liquefaction, settlement, and slope considerations;
- allowable bearing, lateral pressure, friction, uplift, settlement, excavation, drainage, and slab recommendations;
- foundation reuse criteria and new footing/pier design parameters;
- construction observation and special inspection requirements.

Acceptance: the structural engineer can design each support and the contractor can price excavation without inventing soil parameters.

## 5. Architectural design package

Required content:

- site plan, demolition plan, dimensioned floor plans, roof plan, reflected ceiling/framing coordination where useful;
- exterior elevations and building sections with grades and controlling heights;
- enlarged plans and details for balcony/loading opening, sliding door, jib/hoist interface, stairs or ladder, guards, and roof transitions;
- door, window, room/use, finish, and equipment schedules;
- exterior materials/colors and relationship to the house/outbuilding;
- renderings clearly labeled as illustrative;
- accessibility, egress, natural light/ventilation, guards, and life-safety coordination appropriate to the legal use.

Acceptance: geometry agrees across plans, elevations, sections, schedules, CAD models, and structural grids.

## 6. Structural engineering package

Required content:

- existing-structure assessment and limits on reliance upon existing walls/slab/foundations;
- design codes, risk category, material specifications, deflection/vibration criteria, durability and corrosion protection;
- dead, live, storage, concentrated equipment, hoist/trolley, roof, rain, construction, wind, seismic, and any applicable snow loads;
- load combinations, tributary areas, load take-down, reactions, and foundation demands;
- global stability model and independent truss/beam/column checks with compatible connection forces;
- member schedule, truss geometry, bracing, diaphragms, collectors, chords, connections, base plates, anchors, welds/bolts, and foundations;
- temporary stability, erection sequence, shoring, and existing-roof-removal requirements;
- special inspections, testing, delegated design, and deferred-submittal criteria;
- signed and sealed calculations and drawings for permit/construction issue.

Acceptance: loads trace continuously from roof, walls, floor, equipment, and hoist to verified supports and soil; calculations and drawings use the same geometry and member names.

## 7. Envelope and building science package

Required content:

- roof assembly beneath/around building-integrated or rackless-looking PV components;
- low hip-cap framing interface, underlayment, metal roofing, flashings, edges, hips, penetrations, and maintenance access;
- drainage slopes, gutters/scuppers/downspouts, discharge locations, and overflow provisions;
- metal-panel wall assembly, attachment, water/air control layers, insulation, thermal bridging, vapor/condensation analysis, and interior finish;
- soffit/fascia, balcony/walkway waterproofing, doors/windows, transitions to existing work, and sealant schedule;
- ventilation strategy for roof cavities and loft; fire-resistance and ignition-resistant requirements where applicable;
- manufacturer data, warranties, compatibility, and mockup/testing requirements.

Acceptance: every exterior transition has a drainable, flashable, buildable detail with compatible products.

## 8. Solar, electrical, and energy package

Required content:

- surveyed roof azimuth, tilt, usable area, obstructions, and shade study;
- module layout with active and visually matching inactive infill panels identified;
- annual/hourly production model, assumptions, degradation, losses, and sensitivity;
- consumption/tariff data, PV/ESS sizing, export assumptions, economics, and current utility rules;
- electrical service/load study, one-line, conductor/conduit schedule, grounding/bonding, rapid shutdown, disconnects, labels, fire pathways, equipment locations, and vehicle-impact protection;
- structural attachment or integrated-roof interface and waterproofing coordination;
- utility interconnection, City permit documents, commissioning, monitoring, and operations plan.

Acceptance: array geometry matches the roof model; electrical, structural, fire-access, and waterproofing requirements are coordinated.

## 9. MEP and life safety

Required content:

- lighting, receptacles, dedicated equipment circuits, service changes, controls, and exterior-rated equipment;
- ventilation and any heating/cooling requirements based on legal use and enclosure;
- plumbing or roof-drainage connections, if any;
- egress route, doors, stairs/ladder, guards/handrails, fall protection, smoke/CO detection, and fire separation;
- fire extinguisher and emergency shutoff/labeling provisions where applicable.

Acceptance: systems are coordinated with structure and enclosure and can be permitted, installed, accessed, and maintained.

## 10. Construction planning, cost, and procurement

Required content:

- demolition and build-around-existing sequence;
- erection plan, temporary bracing/shoring, lifting access, working clearances, and weather protection;
- protection of occupants, neighbors, landscaping, utilities, retained garage contents, and public way;
- inspection/test plan and hold points;
- quantity takeoff, estimate by trade, design allowances, contingency, bid alternates, exclusions, and escalation date;
- baseline schedule, permitting duration, long-lead equipment, fabrication/shop-drawing sequence, and procurement register;
- contractor/fabricator constructability review and value-engineering record.

Acceptance: bidders price the same scope and the proposed sequence does not depend on unsupported existing construction.

## 11. Issued and record documents

Required content:

- frozen permit and construction issue sets with revision and purpose;
- calculations, specifications, forms, agency approvals, plan-check comments/responses, and deferred submittals;
- shop drawings, product data, samples, RFIs, substitutions, field reports, special inspections, and test results;
- change orders and record of design changes;
- as-built drawings/models, photographs, commissioning reports, warranties, maintenance instructions, and final approvals.

Acceptance: a third party can reconstruct what was approved, what was built, and how to operate and maintain it.
