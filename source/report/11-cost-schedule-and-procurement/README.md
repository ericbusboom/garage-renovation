# 11 — Cost, Schedule, and Procurement

This section turns the coordinated design into comparable bid scopes and a practical project sequence. Estimates must state their date, quantities, labor assumptions, allowances, exclusions, taxes, escalation, contingency, and design maturity.

## Required outputs

- quantity takeoff and estimate by trade
- structural/fabrication alternatives and value-engineering record
- bid scope and alternates
- design, permit, procurement, fabrication, and construction schedule
- long-lead and procurement register
