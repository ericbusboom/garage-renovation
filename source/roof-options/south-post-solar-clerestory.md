# South-post solar slope study

The current CAD model places the T-SO / outer south post line 63 inches south of the existing south wall. This study starts a 30-degree roof at that post line, at a 9-foot low edge.

For the 325-square-foot central version:

- Full east-west width: 23′-5½″
- Slope length: 13′-10¼″
- Horizontal run: 12′-0″
- Vertical rise: 6′-11⅛″
- Slope top: 15′-11⅛″ above grade
- Slope termination: 6′-9″ north of the existing south wall
- Loft-floor top: 9′-8″
- Height above loft at slope top: 6′-3⅛″
- Clerestory rise to an 8-foot envelope: 1′-8⅞″
- Hip-cap eave: 17′-8″ above grade
- Illustrated hip-cap rise: 1'-6"
