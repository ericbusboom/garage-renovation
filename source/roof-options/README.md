# West elevation roof studies

- Solar roof width: 23′-5½″
- Required active solar area: 300 ft²
- Required sloped length at full width: 12′-9½″
- 30° horizontal run: 11′-⅞″
- 30° vertical drop: 6′-4¾″
- Roof height after 30° slope: 13′-7¼″
- Remaining south flat length: 9′-8⅛″
- Loft floor top: 9′-8″
- Clear envelope at the low flat roof: 3′-11¼″ before roof structure

Important ambiguity: a 30% grade equals 16.7°, not 30°. At a literal 30% grade, the solar band drops only 3′-8⅛″ and ends at 16′-3⅞″, leaving 6′-7⅞″ above the loft floor before roof structure.
