Chord and floor-beam comparisons — September 9

B2 position / B3 removal comparison PDF · T1 chord and web comparison PDF · Reproducible comparison source package

The latest studies test a heavier T1 upper chord with a W6 lower rail, then remove B3 and transfer north floor support into T-N. The preferred study layout uses one B2 at 161.375 inches north of the south wall, balancing two 91.625-inch joist spans. It uses about 7,286 lb of primary steel plus connection allowance. Two equal-spaced W8×24 beams shorten the joist spans to 61.083 inches but increase primary steel to about 7,641 lb.

The layout comparison retains T1 with W6×16 below, W6×20 above, HSS 2×2×1/8 verticals and HSS 2½×2½×1/8 end diagonals. A separate, lighter HSS-upper-chord option is also documented. Columns remain fixed; no gravity credit is added to the existing walls.

These are conditional gravity comparisons, not construction details or lifting ratings. Secondary joist optimization, actual joint/seat details, bracing, lateral stability and trolley flange checks remain unresolved. The earlier calculation packages and architectural renders remain available below; they have not been redrawn to adopt these comparison layouts.

Truss designs and calculations — revision 3

Open the 21-page truss design and calculation PDF · Download the reproducible Python calculation package

The revised connected-frame study includes T-S, T1, T-E, T-W and T-N as complete trusses, with B2 as a W8×31 beam and T1 using a W8×24 bottom chord. Each truss has a dimensioned member-ID drawing, governing force/capacity calculations, load-transfer actions and lighter-section rejection tests.

The conditional section search reaches about 7,495 lb of primary steel plus connection allowance, a maximum gravity screening ratio of 0.899, and maximum service movement of 0.730 in. It includes a single 1,000 lb lifted load at nine positions on either rail, with provisional equipment and dynamic allowances. This is a local section-search result, not a proven global minimum or a lifting rating.

These are preliminary candidate sizes, not fabrication or construction drawings. Lateral loads, second-order stability, actual bracing and joints, trolley wheel/flange effects and foundations remain unresolved. Roof purlin direction and load-transfer details are explicit assumptions. The earlier HSS-only report below is retained as history; its sizes and reactions do not describe this revised configuration.

Preliminary structural analysis

Open the eight-page structural review PDF · Download the Python model, inputs and results

The first-order gravity study estimates 20,443 lb of permanent construction weight and 55,747 lb total downward load in the perimeter-storage service case. N2 carries about 15,000 lb in that case. The analysis gives no added gravity credit to the existing walls, slab or cabinets.

The PDF includes the load budget, column reactions, trial HSS sections, joint/stiffness sensitivity and a concentrated-load floor check. These are preliminary trial sections, not construction sizing: connections, lateral stability and foundations remain unresolved. S1 has no upper-frame connection in the current model; S3 is assumed active and also tested as absent. The source package preserves the exact model inputs and calculation outputs.

Exterior views

Southwest — solar roof and west balcony

The latest roof rises at 30° from the south outer beam, then meets a shallow four-sided hip cap at the back. The cap rises 18″ above its eaves and has an 8″ overhang, white soffit and 8″ fascia. Solar and matching infill panels form a flush surface. The west walkway slats are horizontal, with a white balcony floor at the loft doorway.

Northwest — north loft door and west wall

The north loft door is centered one-quarter of the garage-door width from its west edge. Windows flank it. The west wall uses the one-window option beside the French doors.

Exposed steel with interior metal panels — alternate finish

An alternate architectural finish where steel remains exposed and painted dark metal, with light gray 2-inch insulated infill panels mounted inside the frame. Vertical panel joints are spaced 24 inches apart with small expressed seams. West balcony glazing is recessed, with a white balcony floor and the frame visible at the outer edge. These are visualization studies — panel attachments, weatherproof transitions, and lateral bracing are not detailed.

Loft floor dimensions

The current model’s main deck is 18′ 8¼″ east–west × 15′ 3″ north–south, approximately 285 sq ft, excluding the west balcony. Its east edge ends at T-E. The 23′ 5½″ dimension belongs to the full north truss, which also spans the west walkway; it is not the main floor width. The green outline and dimension arrows on the plan now identify the modeled floor edges.
