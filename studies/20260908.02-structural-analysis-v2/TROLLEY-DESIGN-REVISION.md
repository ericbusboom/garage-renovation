# Trolley beam design revision — pending reanalysis

Owner requests W (wide-flange I-shaped) sections for B2 and the bottom beam of T1, to carry a light-duty trolley with a suspended load of a few hundred pounds.

Interpretation: B2 becomes a W-section beam. T1 remains the full-height truss in the approved framing arrangement; its bottom chord becomes a W-section trolley rail. Do not replace the complete T1 truss with a shallow beam without a separate layout decision.

The existing PDF/results describe the earlier square-HSS candidate study. They do not qualify these W-section replacements or any lifting capacity. No W size has been selected yet.

Required next calculation: actual trolley/hoist dead weight; owner-confirmed rated lifted load; moving wheel loads and their spacing; applicable dynamic and lateral effects; simultaneous floor/storage loading; bottom-flange local bending; torsion and lateral restraint; deflection; truss bottom-chord combined axial and bending demand; joints, splices, end stops and unobstructed trolley travel. Check actual trolley compatibility with flange width, thickness and profile. Do not transfer the HSS section properties to a W-section solver unchanged: W sections have different major/minor-axis stiffness and torsional behavior.

Owner-confirmed rated lifted load: 1,000 lb. Hoist/trolley mass is additional and remains unselected. Dynamic effects are additional design actions. Assess each rail for this lifted load at all trolley positions; simultaneous use of multiple hoists has not been specified.
