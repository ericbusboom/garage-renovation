NEZHA / QWIIC ADAPTER - REV B MILLING INPUTS
J1: Amphenol 73306-111LF (latch up). J2 pin 1 / GND is at the bottom
in the component-side drawing. Do not use revision A tooling for this board.
These are design inputs, not machine G-code.
Single-sided copper stock, component/copper side up. Nominal thickness 1.40 mm.
Finished outline: 44.00 x 24.00 mm; apply tool-radius compensation in CAM.
RS-274X front copper + outline; metric Excellon non-plated drills.
Origin: upper-left board corner. X = 0..44 mm; Y = 0..-24 mm.
Do not mirror. Keep copper, outline and drill files aligned to their common origin.
Two 3.00 mm holes at (4,-3) and (40,-21) in exported coordinates.
Tracks 0.40 mm; required copper clearance >=0.30 mm; Qwiic pad gaps 0.40 mm.
Choose an effective isolation cut width below the smallest gap with margin.
Determine speeds, feeds, depth, outline tabs, and panel spacing for actual tools,
stock, machine calibration and fixturing; review simulation and cut a coupon.
Fit the insulated-body 1206 zero-ohm resistor R1: it bridges SCL over SDA.
Check cable-to-header continuity and power shorts before first power.
No physical prototype or machine toolpath has been tested.
