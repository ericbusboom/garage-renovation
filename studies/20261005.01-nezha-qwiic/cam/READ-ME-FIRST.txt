NEZHA / QWIIC - REV B - ONE BOARD - ORIGINAL CARVERA ATC

PROGRAM: nezha-qwiic-revB-one-board-carvera-cut145.nc
J1 is AMPHENOL 73306-111LF. This revision replaces the Kycon jack layout.
J2 pin 1 / GND is now at the bottom in the component-side board drawing.
Use the original Carvera with its six-slot automatic tool changer.
This program does not apply to the Air's manual tool changes.

STOCK AND PLACEMENT
100 x 150 mm, 1.4 mm thick. Place 150 mm left-right along X, 100 mm
front-back along Y. Copper face UP. This is the BARE-COPPER version,
prepared for FR-4 and nominal 35 micrometre copper; no solder-mask removal.
If the stock is coated or has substantially heavier copper, revise the job.
Use your flat sacrificial backer and double-sided tape, approximately 1 mm
thick as reported. Support the board and surrounding blank evenly. Avoid soft
foam tape that lets the board move under probing or cutting pressure.
Secure the backer and blank;
keep clamps and screws out of the cutting AND dust-shoe travel area.
The finished board occupies X15..59, Y15..39 mm of the stock.
The cutter sweeps approximately X14.2..59.8, Y14.2..39.8 mm.
Keep extra clearance for the dust shoe; its envelope is larger than the cutter.

LOAD THESE TOOLS
Slot 2: standard 30-degree V-bit with a 0.2 mm flat tip, 1/8-inch shank.
Slot 3: standard 0.8 mm corn bit, 1/8-inch shank (not a 0.8 mm drill).
Fit the proper Carvera collars. Leave the wireless probe in its normal holder.
The program selects T2 and T3 automatically and starts/stops the spindle.
Confirm the tool labels and diameters when loading; a 0.1/0.3 mm tip, different
V angle, or different corn-bit diameter requires different toolpaths.

ORIGIN AND PROBING IN CARVERA CONTROLLER
1. Home the machine normally. Upload/open the NC and inspect its preview.
2. Set G54 XY zero to the LOWER-LEFT CORNER OF THE STOCK, as viewed from above.
   If that corner coincides with the configured anchor-1 datum, use anchor 1
   with X/Y offsets of zero. Otherwise use offsets that locate that actual corner.
   The NC ALREADY places the board 15 mm in from both edges. Do not add 15 again.
3. Set Z zero to the TOP COPPER SURFACE using Auto Z Probe. Use an unobstructed
   point on the blank, for example work-coordinate X37 Y27 (center of the board).
   If choosing 'Work Origin' probing in the dialog, enter those probe offsets.
4. Enable Scan Margin and Auto Leveling. Use 6 X points and 4 Y points over the
   machining area, with 2 mm probing lift, approximately 9 x 8 mm spacing.
   The probe grid must cover the whole X14.6..59.4, Y14.6..39.4 tool-center area
   and lie on the blank. Observe the scan and ensure the probe/dust shoe clear
   the clamps and bracket. Probe and level the newly mounted stock before running.
5. The program preserves the controller's G54 and native height compensation.
   It does not set XY/Z zero, home the machine, or collect a height map itself.
   Keep leveling active for the entire combined job. Do not apply a second
   external auto-level correction to the same NC.

CUTTING SEQUENCE
T2: isolate copper, 12,000 RPM, 300 mm/min XY, 100 mm/min plunge,
    Z = -0.08 mm. Nominal effective V-bit width at this depth is 0.2429 mm.
T3: pocket the two 3.00 mm holes, removing the centers completely.
T3: cut the outline LAST, 12,000 RPM, 300 mm/min XY, 100 mm/min plunge,
    maximum 0.25 mm stepdown, final Z = -1.45 mm.
The final depth extends only 0.05 mm below the nominal 1.4 mm PCB, into the
adhesive layer. Tape thickness is NOT added to the programmed cutting depth.
With about 1 mm of tape, the cutter should remain within the tape if the board
and tape stay flat and supported. Auto-leveling follows the top surface; it
does not measure PCB thickness, its underside, or tape compression.
The earlier 2 mm recommendation described backer thickness, not extra cut depth.
Use this REV B program for the Amphenol jack. REV A programs fit the old jack.
Four tabs remain: one centered on each edge, minimum neck width 2.5 mm,
remaining thickness 0.5 mm. Roots are wider because the cutter ends are rounded.
General clearance is Z5; end-of-job retraction is Z10. Automatic vacuum mode
is enabled with M331. Keep the enclosure shut and dust collection operating.

AFTER MACHINING
Wait for the spindle to stop. Remove the blank, cut the four tabs with the small
saw, and dress the edges. Inspect/deburr the isolation cuts without removing
fine pads. Before assembly/power, confirm no shorts between signal nets.
Fit R1, the insulated-body 1206 zero-ohm resistor, and continuity-check the
actual 4P4C cable against the header labels before connecting a sensor.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS
The NC passed offline checks for tool slots, command syntax, clearance moves,
stock/depth bounds, retained copper, separated/connected signal nets, complete
hole pockets, and four retaining tabs. See validation.json and setup-preview.pdf.
This file has not been executed on your physical machine. Cutting accuracy
still depends on actual tools, material, workholding, probing, and calibration.
