# Historical source reports

These original PDFs are preserved unchanged for traceability. They are preliminary studies, not the current Chapter 8 recommendation or an approved design.

- `Garage-Solar-Optimization.pdf`: prior comprehensive analysis using about 401 ft² of solar face; its 6–7 kW recommendation predates the 325 ft² clerestory concept.
- `Solar-Analysis-Charts.pdf`: original chart compilation from that analysis.
- `south-post-solar-clerestory.pdf`: newer 325 ft² geometry source. Its “active solar plane” label is corrected to gross solar face in SOL-001.
- `west-elevation-roof-options.pdf`: earlier roof alternatives retained to document design development.

The main chapter reproduces every recovered solar chart and both related roof diagrams in a labeled historical gallery. Use `../solar-energy-report.pdf` for the reconciled conclusions.
